From our workshop in Tegalwangi, Cirebon, we preserve ancestral weaving heritage by combining contemporary modern design with world export quality standards.

Our Story & Business Philosophy

Founded by Pak Ridwan Hidayat in 2008, our business began with a commitment to the abundant supply of Indonesia's finest rattan and the craftsmanship of local artisans in Cirebon. Today, Cirebon is recognized as a global rattan craft hub, contributing over 70% of Indonesia's furniture exports.

Pak Ridwan is dedicated to delivering furniture and homeware that are not only aesthetically refined, but also eco-friendly, durable for decades, and free from wood pests through kiln-dry seasoning and international-standard fumigation.
